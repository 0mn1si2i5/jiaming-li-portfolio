## Product problem

Digital globes often make one dataset easier to browse, but they rarely help people compare different ways of understanding the same place. Mundus turns one navigable Earth into three public modes—spatial, human, and temporal—so a visitor can move from geometric relationships to development patterns and sunlight without learning three separate products.

## Platform capability

The modes share one WebGL globe, geographic interaction model, and bilingual interface. Each registered, versioned extension declares its state and data dependencies; meaningful inputs can be reopened from a versioned URL. A data registry records sources, licenses, transformations, hashes, missing-value policies, and boundary caveats. A new lens reuses the scene, camera, picking, provenance, validation, quality loading, and raster fallback, keeping new questions focused on their evidence instead of rebuilding the globe.

</section>

<section lang="zh-CN" data-locale-content="zh">

## 产品问题

数字地球通常只是让一份数据更容易浏览，却很少帮助人们比较理解同一地点的不同方式。Mundus 将同一个可导航地球组织为空间、人类与时间三种公开模式，让访客可以从球面关系转向发展结构和日照变化，而不必学习三个彼此割裂的产品。

## 平台能力

三种模式共用一套 WebGL 地球、地理交互模型和双语界面。每个注册式、带版本的扩展都声明自身状态与数据依赖，关键输入可以通过带版本的 URL 重新打开。数据注册表记录来源、许可、转换、哈希、缺失值策略与边界说明。新的观察方式复用场景、相机、拾取、来源记录、验证、质量加载和栅格回退，让新问题聚焦于自身证据，而不是重建地球。

</section>

## User value

Other Side makes a distant spherical relationship tangible. Development, Unpacked reveals how similar aggregate outcomes can hide different health, education, and income structures. Sunline makes changing daylight and civil twilight explorable through time. Together they turn abstract geographic evidence into observations a visitor can inspect, revisit, and share.

## Extensible by design

The current public results remain educational: dataset scope, cartographic boundaries, and solar approximations are presented as limits rather than hidden behind visual certainty.

</section>

<section lang="zh-CN" data-locale-content="zh">

## 用户价值

“地球另一端”让遥远的球面关系变得直观；“发展的不同侧面”揭示相近综合结果下不同的健康、教育与收入结构；“日照线”让访客沿时间探索白昼与民用曙暮光。三者共同把抽象地理证据变成可以检查、重访与分享的观察。

## 为扩展而设计

当前公开结果保持教育用途定位：数据集范围、制图边界与太阳近似都作为明确限制呈现，而不会被视觉确定性掩盖。

</section>
