## What I am solving

World knowledge is usually split across maps, statistical tables, astronomy tools, and data portals. Mundus is a personal digital globe I maintain over time: one geographic context where I can add governed ways of looking without rebuilding the product around every dataset.

The current modes are the first three observation lenses, not a final catalog. They establish the shared globe, place state, sources, methods, bilingual interface, and accessible controls that later views must reuse.

## How the product works

A visitor chooses a lens, manipulates the same globe and place, receives a specific result, opens its method, source, year, and limits, then shares the current URL or changes lenses without losing geographic context.

- **Other Side** starts from one location, calculates its antipode, and makes the two endpoints and their represented major-city relationships tangible.
- **Development, Unpacked** shows reported HDI beside derived health, education, and income structure, with year, median, history, source, and missing states.
- **Sunline** turns a UTC time into solar position, daylight, twilight, sunrise, and sunset estimates on the same globe.

## 我在解决什么问题

现实中的世界知识通常分散在地图、统计表、天文工具和数据门户中。Mundus 是我长期维护的一颗个人数字地球：它保留同一个地理上下文，让我可以持续增加经过治理的观察方式，而不必围绕每份数据重新搭建产品。

当前模式是最早落地的三个观察视角，不是最终目录。它们建立了后续视角必须复用的共享地球、地点状态、来源与方法说明、中英双语界面和无障碍控件。

## 当前产品如何工作

访客先选择一个观察视角，再操作同一颗地球和地点，得到一个明确结果，查看方法、来源、年份与限制，最后分享当前 URL，或在不丢失地理上下文的情况下切换视角。

- **地球另一端**从一个地点出发，计算对跖点，并让两端位置及其收录主要城市关系变得直观。
- **发展的不同侧面**把已发布 HDI 与派生的健康、教育和收入结构并置，同时显示年份、中位数、历史变化、来源与缺失状态。
- **日照线**把一个 UTC 时间转换为同一颗地球上的太阳位置、白昼、曙暮光与近似日出日落。

## What V1.1 delivered

Parchment Atlas moved the public product from a dark laboratory treatment to a warmer digital exhibition. Other Side now opens a draggable through-Earth cross-section. GeoNames supports English and Chinese major-city search, marks canonical-name fallbacks instead of inventing translations, and shows represented city relationships at both endpoints. Natural Earth 110m/50m vector geometry gives the shared globe a bounded, reproducible cartographic surface.

The result remains a static client that can be built, verified, and published under the GitHub Pages project path. It is the shipped V1.1 product, not a candidate description.

## Product and technical decisions

A single globe kernel keeps place, camera behavior, controls, and sharing consistent across lenses. Static delivery avoids accounts, telemetry, and server maintenance. Reviewed data snapshots and hashes prevent upstream changes from silently altering results; larger assets load only when a lens needs them. English, Chinese, keyboard use, visible focus, reduced motion, and WebGL fallback make the product testable beyond my own machine.

Pages subpath checks and artifact verification exist for the same reason: a public version should be reproducible, not merely work in a local development session.

## Boundaries and future

Mundus is not street navigation, a professional GIS, or an authoritative scientific service. The first three observation lenses can be followed by new views or governed data overlays, but each addition must pass data identity, method transparency, performance, accessibility, and release verification.

It is not a plugin platform and has no third-party runtime plugin system. GHSL Human Morphology did not pass global validation and is not a public feature. “Maintaining my own globe” describes a long-term product direction, not a claim that every view of Earth is already present.

## V1.1 做了什么

Parchment Atlas 将公开产品从深色实验室视觉转向更温暖的数字展览。“地球另一端”现在可以打开并拖拽穿过地球的剖面。GeoNames 支持中英文主要城市搜索，在缺少中文名时明确标记原名 fallback，而不是编造翻译，并显示起点与对跖点两侧的收录城市关系。Natural Earth 110m/50m 矢量几何为共享地球提供有边界、可复现的制图表面。

产品仍是可以在 GitHub Pages 项目子路径下构建、验证和发布的纯静态客户端。这些内容是已经上线的 V1.1，而不是候选版本说明。

## 关键产品与技术决策

单一地球内核让不同视角共享地点、相机行为、控件和分享方式。静态交付避免账号、遥测和服务器维护。经过审核的数据快照与哈希避免上游变化悄悄改写结果，较大的资源只在对应视角需要时加载。中英文、键盘操作、可见焦点、reduced motion 和 WebGL fallback 让产品不只在作者电脑上成立。

Pages 子路径检查与制品验证服务于同一个目标：公开版本必须可复现，而不是只在本地开发环境中可用。

## 边界与未来

Mundus 不是街道导航、专业 GIS 或权威科学服务。最早落地的三个观察视角之后可以继续增加新的观察方式或经过治理的数据叠层，但每项扩展都必须通过数据身份、方法透明度、性能、无障碍与发布验证。

它现在不是插件平台，也没有第三方运行时插件系统。GHSL Human Morphology 尚未通过全球验证，不是公开功能。“维护一颗自己的地球”是长期产品方向，不代表所有观察方式已经完成。
