## A personal globe in use today

Mundus began with a practical question: how could I keep different kinds of world knowledge in the same geographic context? It is now a public personal globe where a visitor can choose a way of looking, work with a place, read the result with its source and method, and share that state.

## Three ways to read the same place

- **Other Side** follows a selected point through Earth to its antipode and relates the represented cities at both ends.
- **Development, Unpacked** places published HDI beside health, education, and income dimensions, with year, history, source, and missing states.
- **Sunline** relates UTC time to solar position, daylight, twilight, and the day-night boundary.

The three modes keep the same globe and location context. Moving between them changes the question while preserving the place under examination.

</section>

<section lang="zh-CN" data-locale-content="zh">

## 一颗正在使用的个人数字地球

Mundus 起始于一个具体问题：怎样让不同类型的世界知识始终处在同一个地理上下文中？如今，它是一颗已经公开的个人数字地球。访客可以选择观察方式，操作地点，阅读结果及其来源与方法，再分享当前状态。

## 从同一地点进入三种观察方式

- **地球另一端**沿地心找到所选地点的对跖点，并呈现两端收录城市之间的关系。
- **发展的不同侧面**把已发布 HDI 与健康、教育、收入维度放在一起，同时给出年份、历史变化、来源与缺失状态。
- **日照线**把 UTC 时间与太阳位置、白昼、曙暮光和昼夜边界联系起来。

三个模式共用同一颗地球和地点上下文。切换模式会改变问题，同时保留正在观察的位置。

</section>

## One system beneath the views

A shared globe and location state keep the modes coherent. Reviewed data snapshots and hashes keep published results stable, while static delivery makes the public build reproducible.

Working on that spatial system came from an earlier and more difficult creative-system idea.

</section>

<section lang="zh-CN" data-locale-content="zh">

## 三种视角下的同一套空间系统

共享的地球与地点状态让三个模式保持连贯。经过审核的数据快照和哈希用于稳定公开结果，静态交付则让线上构建可以复现。

这套空间系统的实践，来自一个更早、也更困难的创作构想。

</section>

## Chronorbis: time, events, and place

I have long wanted to make an alternate-history authoring simulator. In that system, every event, institution, and character would belong to specific times and places.

Chronorbis was the name of an earlier worldbuilding and narrative architecture experiment. I imagined storing slices of the world in a persistent, editable knowledge base, then assigning built-in multi-agent roles to research material, maintain characters and events, and write under the constraints of that world's context.

The full system remains technically unresolved. Mundus is my decision to make the spatial part concrete first: one globe, explicit locations, traceable data, and interactions that can be used as a real product. What I learn from maintaining that space may inform Chronorbis later. Chronorbis remains a separate, unfinished direction.

## Chronorbis：让时间、事件与地点共存

我一直希望制作一套架空历史创作模拟器。在这个系统里，每个事件、制度与人物都应当发生在具体的时间与空间中。

Chronorbis 是我早期进行世界观与叙事架构实验时使用的名称。我曾设想把世界观切片存入长期保存、随时编辑的知识库，再通过内置 multi-agent 分工处理资料研究、人物与事件维护，并让创作严格服从这个世界已有的上下文。

完整系统所需的技术条件至今仍未解决。Mundus 是我先把空间部分落实下来的选择：维护一颗地球，明确地点，记录数据来源，并把交互做成真正可用的产品。维护这套空间系统得到的经验，未来或许会帮助我重新思考 Chronorbis。Chronorbis 仍是一条独立且尚未实现的方向。
