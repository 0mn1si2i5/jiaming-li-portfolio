## How it plays

NBTI is a playful test that uses historical figures as a reference rather than as a personality diagnosis. It is mobile-first and direct: people move through 18 concrete scenarios and six calibration questions, then receive a result built from six action tendencies—a primary historical counterpart, nearby and contrasting matches, a short reading, and links to the source material behind the historical notes.

## How matching works

I designed the product, interaction flow, visual direction, and the matching system, then built it as a static React site. A deterministic distance model compares each response vector with 32 character profiles, so every result is generated locally and repeatably—without an account, cookies, analytics, or saved answers.

## Boundaries

NBTI is a playful cultural experience, not a psychological or scientific assessment. The historical figures are loose points of comparison rather than proof of a personality label; the result writing keeps the user’s present choices in focus and treats the past as material for reflection, not a verdict.

## 玩法

NBTI 是一场把历史人物当作参照的趣味测试，而非对人格的诊断。产品以移动端体验为核心：用户完成 18 个具体情境与 6 道校准题后，会得到基于六种行动倾向生成的结果，包括主要历史人物参照、相近与对照人物、简短解读和史料来源链接。

## 如何匹配

我负责产品概念、交互流程、视觉方向和匹配系统，并将其实现为静态 React 网站。确定性距离模型会将回答向量与 32 个人物档案比较，因此结果可在本地重复生成；产品不要求账户、不使用 Cookie 或分析追踪，也不保存回答。

## 使用边界

NBTI 是文化与历史导向的互动体验，不是心理测评或科学诊断。历史人物仅作为理解选择的参照；结果应被视为反思材料，而非人格结论。
